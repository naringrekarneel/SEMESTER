# Explain Probabilistic Index Term Weighting and Compare it with TF-IDF (10 Marks)

## Introduction

In Information Retrieval (IR), **term weighting** determines how important a word (term) is in a document. Good term weighting improves the ranking of relevant documents. Two popular methods are **TF-IDF** and **Probabilistic Index Term Weighting**.

---

# Probabilistic Index Term Weighting

## Definition

**Probabilistic Index Term Weighting** assigns weights to terms based on the **probability that a document containing the term is relevant** to a user's query.

Unlike TF-IDF, which uses only term frequency and document frequency, probabilistic weighting uses **relevance information** (whether documents are relevant or non-relevant).

---

## Idea (ELI5)

Imagine you are searching for **"machine learning"** books.

* Word **"learning"** appears in almost every book.
* Word **"backpropagation"** appears mostly in books that are actually about machine learning.

Since **backpropagation** appears more often in relevant books, it receives a **higher weight**.

---

## Formula

The Robertson-Sparck Jones (RSJ) probabilistic weight is

$$
W_i=\log\left(\frac{p_i(1-u_i)}{u_i(1-p_i)}\right)
$$

Where:

* $p_i$ = Probability that term $i$ occurs in relevant documents.
* $u_i$ = Probability that term $i$ occurs in non-relevant documents.

---

### When relevance information is unavailable

The weight is approximated as

$$
W_i=\log\left(\frac{N-n_i+0.5}{n_i+0.5}\right)
$$

Where:

* $N$ = Total number of documents
* $n_i$ = Number of documents containing term $i$

This approximation is widely used in **BM25**.

---

## Working Steps

1. User submits a query.
2. Estimate which documents are relevant.
3. Calculate probability of each term appearing in relevant documents.
4. Calculate probability of each term appearing in non-relevant documents.
5. Assign higher weights to terms that better distinguish relevant documents.
6. Rank documents based on these weights.

---

## Advantages

* Uses **relevance feedback**.
* Usually provides **better retrieval accuracy** than TF-IDF.
* Forms the basis of modern ranking methods like **BM25**.
* More effective for large search engines.

---

## Disadvantages

* Requires relevance information or estimates.
* More computationally complex.
* Difficult to estimate probabilities accurately.

---

# TF-IDF vs Probabilistic Index Term Weighting

| Feature                  | TF-IDF                       | Probabilistic Weighting  |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Principle                | Frequency-based              | Probability-based        |
| Uses relevance feedback  | No                           | Yes                      |
| Main factors             | TF and IDF                   | Probability of relevance |
| Complexity               | Simple                       | More complex             |
| Accuracy                 | Good                         | Usually better           |
| Popular models           | Vector Space Model           | BM25, Probabilistic IR   |
| Depends on user feedback | No                           | Yes (or estimated)       |
| Best suited for          | Small and medium collections | Large search engines     |

---

# Example

Suppose two terms:

* **"computer"** appears in almost every document.
* **"neural"** appears mainly in documents relevant to the query.

### TF-IDF

* "computer" gets a lower IDF because it is common.
* "neural" gets a higher IDF because it is rarer.

### Probabilistic Weighting

* If **"neural"** appears in **90% of relevant documents** and only **10% of non-relevant documents**, it receives a **very high weight**.
* If **"computer"** appears equally in relevant and non-relevant documents, it receives a **low weight**.

Thus, probabilistic weighting better identifies terms that truly indicate relevance.

---

# Applications

* Web search engines
* Digital libraries
* Enterprise search systems
* BM25 document ranking
* Information Retrieval systems with relevance feedback

---

# Conclusion

Probabilistic Index Term Weighting assigns term importance based on the **likelihood that a term appears in relevant documents**, making it more effective than TF-IDF when relevance information is available. While TF-IDF is simple and widely used, probabilistic weighting generally produces **more accurate document ranking** and serves as the foundation for advanced retrieval models such as **BM25**.
